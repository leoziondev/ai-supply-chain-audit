# Evidence Levels

Use these labels consistently.

- `alegacao`: public report, threat intel, forum, social media or news without validated sample.
- `amostra verificada`: sanitized sample checked against internal systems without sharing personal data.
- `confirmacao interna`: logs, audit trail, registry metadata or internal records confirm access, copy, execution or exfiltration.
- `confirmacao oficial`: public statement, regulatory notice or formal controller decision.
- `incidente descartado`: investigation shows false, recycled, enriched or unrelated data.
- `N/A`: not a data breach or incident-confidence question.

Rules:

- Do not claim compromise based only on an allegation.
- Do not include real personal data, dumps or links to dumps.
- Separate technical facts, hypotheses, regulatory risk and communication risk.
- Mark what would confirm or refute the hypothesis.
